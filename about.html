Our Story

Where heritage meets modern luxury

Home/Our Story

Est. 2018, Jaipur

Born from the Pink City

Jewel was founded in 2018 by Aishwarya Rao, a gemologist and third-generation jeweller from Jaipur. After years of watching beloved heirloom pieces lose their lustre, Aishwarya set out to create jewellery that would remain as radiant on the hundredth wearing as it was on the first.

The name "Jewel" is a Sanskrit exclamation of wonder — the feeling we want every customer to experience when they open their Jewel parcel. Each piece is individually crafted by our team of master artisans in our Jaipur atelier, using responsibly sourced metals and gemstones.

Our proprietary 7-layer anti-tarnish process — developed over three years of research — is what sets Jewel apart. It means your jewellery doesn't just look beautiful on day one; it continues to shine through years of daily wear, through monsoons and celebrations, through every chapter of your story.

What We Stand For

Our Values

✦

Eternal Brilliance

Our 7-layer anti-tarnish treatment is not a coating — it's a transformation. Every Jewel piece undergoes 72 hours of treatment before it leaves our atelier, guaranteeing lustre that lasts years, not months.

🌿

Ethical Sourcing

We work exclusively with certified responsible suppliers for all metals and gemstones. Our supply chain is transparent, traceable, and fully audited — because beautiful jewellery should never come at a hidden cost.

🤲

Master Artisanship

Every AHO piece is handcrafted by artisans with decades of experience. We pay above industry rates and provide full healthcare and education support for our 48-person atelier team in Jaipur.

📦

Luxury Unboxing

The AHO experience begins at the door. Every order arrives in our signature ivory gift box, with a handwritten care card, anti-tarnish pouch, and certificate of authenticity.

♻️

Sustainable Practice

Our packaging is 100% recyclable. We offset all shipping emissions through certified reforestation programmes, and our atelier runs on 60% solar energy.

💎

Lifetime Guarantee

We stand behind every piece we make. If your Jewel jewellery tarnishes within 2 years of normal wear, we will re-treat, repair, or replace it — no questions asked.
